I was the Principal Designer, working with a broader team of 5 (Software Engineer, Tech Lead, Innovation Consultant, Product Owner, Initiative Sponsor)

600+ pages of technical expertise

Field technicians working in Fire & Safety were struggling with lengthy documentation (600+ pages), that was not in their native language. They had to look through hundreds of pages to find the appropriate regulation for their need, or ask overloaded senior technicians for guidance.

We built a Proof of Concept in 4 weeks.

I started with a discovery phase, conducting user interviews, building a prototype, testing it, and improving it accordingly. I collaborated with a software engineer to make it real and ready for deployment.

Awarded product

In 2024, the product was nominated and received an innovation award. It led the way for many other AI-powered initiatives for our customer.
