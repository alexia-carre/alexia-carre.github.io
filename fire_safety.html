01 Context

600+ pages, and not even in their language

Fire & Safety field technicians needed the right regulation, fast. Instead, they had to dig through 600+ pages of documentation that wasn’t in their native language, or ask senior technicians who were already overloaded.

High stakes: in a regulatory context, accuracy, transparency and explainability were non-negotiable.

02 What I did

From interviews to a working PoC in 4 weeks

I led the design from start to finish:

  • Discovery first: 10 user interviews
  • A prototype, tested and improved round after round
  • Hand in hand with a software engineer to make it real and ready for deployment

03 Results

Awarded, and the first of many

40+ technicians use it. In 2024, it was nominated for an innovation award, and won it! It also opened the door to many other AI-powered initiatives for our customer.
