01 Context

Hundreds of thousands of documents, sorted by hand

Compliance officers had to classify technical documents manually. Nothing binary here: each one came with a wide range of parameters and required deep domain expertise. And the work was repetitive, which weighed on them in the long run.

02 What I did

Testing the tech and the business case at the same time

I ran the discovery (interviews, user journeys, stakeholder mapping) while the team explored the technology. On 500 documents, we had to answer two questions at once:

  • Can AI actually take on this challenge?
  • Is there a real business case?

03 Results

A validated PoC, and UX skeptics on board

Our customer validated the proof of concept, with 80% fewer manual reviews. They especially praised the Discovery work: it nailed their pain points.

A huge win, because at first stakeholders were reluctant to join Design Thinking activities (UX ≠ “aesthetics”!). We ended up with 3 more of them on board.
