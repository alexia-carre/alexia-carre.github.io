I was the Principal Designer, working with a broader team of 5 (Tech Lead/PO, Innovation Product Manager, Back-end AI developer, 2x Front-end developers)

Technical documents with a wide range of parameters (not binary)

Compliance officers were faced with a large number of documents to classify, which required a high-level of domain expertise. It also entails repetitive tasks, which were weighting on users in the long run.

Balancing technical experimentation and business need

For this proof of concept, we needed to balance the discovery activities (interviews, user journeys, staleholders mapping), with the technology exploration. The team needed to make sure, at the same time, that:

  • It was possible for AI to take on this challenge
  • There was a business case

The importance of Advocating

We built a proof of concept that was validated by our customer. They highly appreciated the quality of the Discovery work I had done, emphasizing that it was a correct understanding of their pain points. This was a huge win, as stakeholders were reluctant to participate in Design Thinking activities at the beginning, mixing UX with "aesthetics".
