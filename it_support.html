01 Context

An AI assistant for a whole company

Employees needed IT help in the middle of their workday, without leaving the tools they already use. The company chose to offer it through an AI assistant in Teams.

At this scale, an assistant only works if people understand what it can do, trust its answers, and know how to reach a human when it can’t help.

02 What I did

From user research to a multi-channel AI experience

I led the UX and research strategy from start to finish:

  • Discovery first: 20+ user interviews
  • Customer-facing workshops to align stakeholders on needs and priorities
  • AI design principles to guide how the assistant behaves, and end-to-end user journeys across channels
  • Conversation design and UX writing for ticket creation, live agent transfer and knowledge base access
  • Accessibility documentation and design handover to the development team

03 Results

An assistant that keeps improving

The assistant is now in continuous improvement, shaped by what users say and do. The design principles and journeys give the team a shared base to extend it with new features.
