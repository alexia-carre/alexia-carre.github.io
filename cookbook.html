01 Context

My best friend was getting married!

The grooms are true food lovers, so we wanted to give them something special: a personalized cookbook, printed by a professional printer from our network.

02 What I did

20 contributors, 60 pages

We gathered recipes, ingredients, good-quality photos, tips and tools from 20 contributors, then turned it all into a 60-page book. A LOT of back and forth and iterations!

03 Results

3 print fails, one very happy couple

Print design 101, learned the hard way: resolution, margins, texture, transparency… It’s a full-time job, and I’m proud we made it real. Seeing the grooms’ emotions was worth ALL the hours.
